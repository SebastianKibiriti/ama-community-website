01 / ABOUT A.M.A.

Small updates.
Meaningful impact.

Finding an available water point should not have to involve guesswork.

A.M.A. Community is a community-driven information initiative focused on public water points. Residents can share observations about whether a tap is open or closed, report a newly identified water point, and help keep local information useful.

Our approach starts with something simple: people who live in an area often know what is happening there. Sharing that knowledge can help the whole community.

A community report is an observation, not an official guarantee. Conditions can change. Where possible, check the latest update before travelling.
